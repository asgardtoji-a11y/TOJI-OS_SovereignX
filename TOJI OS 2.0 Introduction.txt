Overview

TOJI OS 2.0 paired with Sovereign X is an enterprise‑grade sovereign AI platform engineered to deliver governed, auditable, and resilient AI deployments across cloud, on‑prem, and constrained edge environments. The platform unifies a hardened control plane (policy, registry, installer, evidence) with a secure data/edge plane (sandboxed runtimes, multimodal inference, pilot fabric) so organizations can safely onboard third‑party applications, enforce policy and charter constraints, and produce tamper‑evident audit evidence for every critical lifecycle event.

Evolution: TOJI OS 0.1 → 1.x → 2.0

TOJI OS 0.1 (Foundational Proofs)

Established core concepts: manifest‑driven app packaging, basic sandboxing, and a minimal registry.

Focused on developer ergonomics and a lightweight runtime for experimentation on edge devices.

TOJI OS 1.x (Operationalization and Governance)

Introduced SBOM generation, signature verification, and initial policy gates.

Added a rudimentary installer and operator to manage app lifecycle in Kubernetes pilots.

Built the first connector templates and a conformance harness for adapter validation.

TOJI OS 2.0 (Production Sovereign Platform) — current release

Full integration with Sovereign X: a policy DSL, deterministic pre‑install simulation, and human approval workflows.

Production‑grade App Registry with signed artifacts, SBOMs, and certification badges.

Installer Service that validates signatures, runs conformance tests, executes policy simulation, and records signed evidence bundles for every lifecycle event.

Evidence Collector and exportable, signed evidence bundles (install, update, action, teardown) for audit and compliance.

Pilot Fabric: on‑demand, isolated pilot instantiation with quotas, RBAC, telemetry, and lifecycle automation.

Multimodal Edge Optimizations: quantized models, memory‑bounded inference profiles, adaptive compute routing, and WASM/OCI runtime options for constrained devices.

Connector Fabric: automated CI→registry→conformance→promotion pipeline and an initial certified connector baseline.

What has been developed (Delivered Capabilities)

Secure App Lifecycle

App manifest schema (capabilities, resource profiles, required policies).

SBOM generation and vulnerability gating.

Artifact signing and verification (cosign‑style workflow planned).

Installer API: publish → simulate → install → update → remove.

Air‑gap bundle generation and offline verification support.

Policy and Governance (Sovereign X integration)

Policy DSL for capability gating, required policy declarations, and approval rules.

Deterministic policy simulation that runs pre‑install and returns actionable violations.

Human approval flows for privileged operations and audit hooks for every decision.

Evidence and Auditability

Signed evidence bundles capturing events, policy traces, artifacts, and provenance metadata.

Exportable, append‑only bundles suitable for compliance reviews and forensic analysis.

Pilot Fabric and Isolation

Templates for pilot instantiation with enforced quotas, network and namespace isolation, and telemetry.

Automated lifecycle management (create, promote, teardown) with cost controls.

Connector Ecosystem and Conformance

Adapter template and conformance harness for consistent connector behavior.

Initial set of certified connectors and an automated promotion pipeline.

Edge and Multimodal Runtime

Memory‑bounded inference profiles, quantized model support, and fallback routing to higher‑capacity nodes.

WASM runtime option for ultra‑constrained devices and container runtime with seccomp/AppArmor for others.

Enterprise Benefits

Regulatory readiness: evidence bundles and SBOMs support audits and regulatory reporting.

Operational safety: pre‑install policy simulation and approval gates reduce risk of unauthorized capability use.

Supply‑chain assurance: artifact signing and planned transparency logs reduce tamper and provenance risk.

Scalable pilot model: controlled, cost‑aware pilot instantiation accelerates partner onboarding and proofs of concept.

Edge resilience: optimized inference profiles and fallback routing maintain service continuity on constrained devices.

Key Operational Considerations

Signing and Key Management: move to HSM/KMS for production signing keys and rotate keys per enterprise policy.

SBOM and Vulnerability Policy: define acceptable risk thresholds and automated remediation paths.

Approval Workflows: map privileged operations to human approvers and audit trails.

Telemetry and Alerting: standardize metrics and alerts for connector health, pilot isolation breaches, and policy violations.

Disaster Recovery: backup evidence stores and registry metadata; define restore and verification procedures.

Acceptance Criteria (Suggested)

All app packages require valid signatures and pass SBOM/vulnerability checks before promotion.

Policy simulation blocks violations; privileged installs require explicit human approval.

100% of lifecycle events produce signed evidence bundles and are exportable.

Multimodal inference meets defined latency and memory SLOs on representative low‑end devices.

Registry and installer support concurrent installs at scale and unlimited on‑demand pilot instantiation with enforced quotas and isolation.

Connectors pass conformance tests and emit standardized telemetry.

Next Steps for Enterprise Adoption

Pilot: run a controlled pilot with 1–3 representative apps and a single sovereign to validate policy, evidence, and runtime SLOs.

Security Review: perform SBOM audits, pentesting, and supply‑chain verification.

Scale Plan: certify initial connector set and scale CI/CD for automated promotion.

Operational Runbooks: create runbooks for install, rollback, evidence export, and incident response.

Governance Charter: define approval roles, policy templates, and compliance reporting cadence.
